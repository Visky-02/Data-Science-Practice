#  E-Commerce Customer Segmentation (RFM Analysis)

##  Project Overview
The objective of this project is to analyze an online retailer's transaction data to segment customers based on their purchasing behavior using RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) modeling. The goal is to identify high-value customers, churn risks, and tailor marketing strategies.

##  Tech Stack
* **Data Processing & RFM Modeling:** Python (Pandas)
* **Data Visualization & Dashboarding:** Power BI

##  Key Business Insights
1. **The "Champions" Group:** A small percentage of highly engaged customers generate the majority of the revenue. These are frequent buyers with high basket sizes.
2. **Churn Risk Detection:** A significant portion of historically high-spending customers have high 'Recency' scores, indicating they haven't purchased recently and are at risk of churning.
3. **Low-Value High-Frequency Segment:** Some customer segments buy frequently but have very low monetary value. These require cross-selling strategies.

##  Strategic Recommendations
* **Loyalty Programs:** Implement VIP loyalty tiers specifically for the 'Champions' segment to retain high CLV (Customer Lifetime Value).
* **Win-Back Campaigns:** Trigger automated, highly personalized discount campaigns for customers in the 'At Risk' segment.
* **Bundle Offers:** For the low-value/high-frequency segment, create product bundles to increase the average order value (AOV).
